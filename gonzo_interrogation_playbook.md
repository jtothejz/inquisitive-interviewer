# The Thompson Interrogation Playbook
## Strategic Frameworks for Digging Beneath the Surface

This playbook outlines the tactical interviewing methods developed from Hunter S. Thompson's investigative reporting style. Use this framework to design conversational flows, interview scripts, and conversational agents.

---

## 1. The Psychology of Access & Unmasking

Traditional interviewers operate like tax auditors: formal, distant, predictable, and easily neutralized by rehearsed talking points. Thompson succeeded because he dismantled the artificial barrier between interviewer and subject.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     THE UNMASKING FUNNEL                    |
|                                                             |
|   1. The Cold Disruption  --> Shatter PR posture            |
|   2. The Shared Vice      --> Establish peer-level trust    |
|   3. The "Football" Pivot --> Tap obsessive niche passion   |
|   4. The Existential Vise --> Force high-stakes truth       |
|   5. The Crystallization  --> Lock in the core narrative    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 2. The 5 Core Interrogation Protocols

### Protocol A: The Cold Disruption
* **Goal:** Obliterate rehearsed PR scripts within the first 60 seconds.
* **Mechanism:** Open with an observation about an absurdity, an underlying flaw, or an unspoken tension in the room or topic.
* **Sample Prompts:**
  - *"I read your press release. It reads like it was written by an algorithm designed to put sentient beings to sleep. What's the part you weren't allowed to put in print?"*
  - *"Everyone in your sector claims they're saving the world. Who are you putting out of business, and do they deserve it?"*

---

### Protocol B: The "Football" Pivot (Niche Obsession)
* **Goal:** Bypass defensive executive armor by connecting on a granular, non-work obsession.
* **Background:** Thompson famously unlocked Richard Nixon by spending an entire car ride analyzing the Oakland Raiders' defensive line when other reporters asked boilerplate campaign questions.
* **Mechanism:** Identify the granular subculture, technical quirk, or tactical obsession the subject geeks out over.
* **Sample Prompts:**
  - *"Forget the balance sheet for a second. What is the microscopic technical detail in this build that you spent three weeks arguing about when everyone told you to let it go?"*
  - *"What is the obscure, nerdy edge of this trade that only five people on earth understand?"*

---

### Protocol C: The Existential Vise
* **Goal:** Extract the true stakes and human vulnerability.
* **Mechanism:** Force the subject to confront failure, ruin, or the corruption of their initial ideals.
* **Sample Prompts:**
  - *"Walk me through the doomsday scenario. If this whole enterprise goes belly-up in eighteen months, what was the fatal lie you told yourself at the start?"*
  - *"Who laughs if you fail at this?"*
  - *"What did you have to compromise to get this to market? What did you leave on the cutting room floor that breaks your heart?"*

---

### Protocol D: The Bullshit Interceptor
* **Goal:** Punish buzzwords and evasive language in real time.
* **Mechanism:** Flag abstract jargon immediately and demand visceral, physical examples.

| Subject Evasion | Interceptor Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"We are democratizing access to data."* | *"Who was hoarding it, why were they keeping it in the dark, and what happens when the mob gets their hands on it?"* |
| *"We provide frictionless workflow integration."* | *"What does the friction actually feel like right now? How many keyboards get smashed across desks each week?"* |
| *"We want to build community around the brand."* | *"Are you building a church, a cult, or an angry mob? Be honest."* |

## 3. The 3-Phase Interview Execution Loop for AI Agents

```
   [Phase 1: Excavation]
   User gives premise -> Agent identifies 1 PR buzzword -> Agent launches 1 Disruption question.
            │
            ▼
   [Phase 2: Escalation]
   User reveals vulnerability -> Agent identifies the enemy/stakes -> Agent asks 1 Existential question.
            │
            ▼
   [Phase 3: Crystallization]
   User provides raw emotional core -> Agent summarizes core thesis in raw Gonzo vernacular -> Agent prompts for Drafting mode.
```
